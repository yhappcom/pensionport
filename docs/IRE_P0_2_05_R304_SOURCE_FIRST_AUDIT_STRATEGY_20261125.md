## Proposed architecture: two actual user turns, source-first audit instead of second full graph regeneration

T1 Research: read entire source, produce typed candidate graph, evidence, unresolved source/visual/audio gaps, financial source checks when possible. Freeze first packet, full source and rubric hashes, journal head. No premature approval.

T2 A (source-first challenge): read original source before exposing T1 body. Freeze a complete source-coordinate review ledger across all cues with independently reviewable meaning obligations, including every material factual assertion, question, recommendation, conditional premise, exception, reported opinion, actual/hypothetical distinction, temporal and numeric meaning. Each obligation has a minimal nucleus and contextual evidence; repetition is linked to an existing obligation only with explicit reason. Unintelligible content must remain OPEN; no silently classifying as nonclaim. Source ledger is not a second independently partitioned graph. Coverage census proves review bookkeeping only, not exhaustiveness. No host/model-state independence claim.

T2 B (bidirectional audit): after source ledger freeze, read first body and check every T1 atom for unsupported content or wrong attribution, scope, date, quantity, condition or certainty. Then check every source obligation for actual recoverability from T1. Exact evidence and explicit judgment in both directions, plus independently enumerated quantities/identifiers (including Korean numeral words where reviewed). Allow 1:N/N:1/N:M only when each elemental proposition and all required attributes remain independently recoverable, preserving conjunction vs disjunction, conditions, scope and speaker. Shared coordinates, duplicate words, broad context, same numeric string and mere agreement are not proof. A reference-only claim may be a duplicate elsewhere, actual omission, unsupported reference claim, or unresolved. Detect real omissions even when T1 has broad direct spans. Keep unknowns unresolved. Audit both source-first ledger and T1; source ledger itself can be defective. Any correction to frozen source ledger is separately appended, reasoned and pinned; initial assessment not rewritten. First results and corrected publication candidates separate.

T2 C: append audit verdicts and optional corrected draft via existing journal lineage. No automatic promotion of first/second; ChatGPT final evidence review required. If full census, all high-risk gaps, or official financial checks cannot finish in turn 2, mark HOLD. Do not simulate additional turns or silently repair to get a PASS.

## Measurement, no threshold relaxation

Retain historical strict 9-axis scores and rubric, not comparable to new operational audit units. For a prospective audit define frozen elementary source obligations as recall denominator and elementary first-packet propositions as precision denominator. Decompose first-packet assertions into checkable obligations including conjunctions and implicit scope, with exact parent lineage; an unsupported extra assertion fails its own proposition. Count each source obligation once, even if repeated or distributed across multiple nodes. Require explicit evidence that all material attributes (9 axes plus typed relations) are retained before credit. Missing condition, uncertainty, extra claim, conflicting attribution or unresolved boundary does not earn a match. Independent precision/recall certification remains unmeasurable in ChatGPT-only setting. Proposed internal thresholds remain >=95% precision, >=98% recall, high-risk missed/unsupported/unresolved obligations zero; all source census and financial/knowledge gates apply additionally. This changes the unit of measurement, not merely an evaluator bugfix; user-approved prospective registration and validation required before any operational qualification. Do not equate a new metric to original scores. Initial frozen packet diagnostic and post-audit candidate quality reported separately. No scores from this design diagnostic.
