# Batch 003 synthesis - videos 21 to 30

Status: 30-video research checkpoint. Sequences 21-30 include provisional chronology where exact publication dates remain unverified.

## Main change in this batch
The framework expanded from account/product selection into an end-to-end operating system:
cash-flow and account infrastructure -> goals -> account wrapper -> asset allocation -> implementation product -> structure/cost/tax checks -> execution mechanics -> rebalancing -> long-term maintenance.

## Repeated principles
1. Separate the account wrapper from the assets held inside it.
2. Separate an asset class from the product used to implement it.
3. Convenience or automation does not remove market or credit risk.
4. Learn product structure before comparing headline yield or return.
5. Time-dependent rules, tax rates, fees and product terms require current verification.
6. Practical execution knowledge is part of risk control.

## New knowledge from 21-30
- TDF adds lifecycle allocation and delegated rebalancing to the pension layer.
- Promissory-note products add issuer-credit analysis to cash management.
- CMA practice adds central-account and linked-account design before security selection.
- Tax analysis establishes a strict distinction between historical proposals and enacted current law.
- Silver reinforces that assets in the same broad category can have different demand and volatility drivers.
- Multi-asset ETFs provide another delegated allocation implementation, distinct from lifecycle TDFs.
- Dividend ETFs require evaluation by diversification, cost, tax and total return, not headline yield alone.
- Bond practice converts credit rating, coupon and maturity into a pre-purchase checklist.
- Beginner stock preparation integrates account setup, capital, market knowledge, information quality and behavioral discipline.
- Stock-market mechanics add order, execution, settlement and balance checks to the implementation layer.

## Retirement integration at 30 videos
Pension savings and IRP remain long-horizon tax wrappers; ISA is a separate tax-efficient bucket rather than a pension substitute. TDF is an implementation option inside long-horizon retirement investing, not a guarantee. National Pension remains conceptually a foundational retirement-income layer, but the early source video still lacks a verified video ID and therefore is not counted as processed.

## Exceptions and unresolved areas
- No systematic retirement withdrawal framework has yet been established from the processed source set.
- Exact ordering for several 21-30 videos remains unresolved.
- Historical tax and trading details must not be reused as current rules without official verification.
- Full transcripts are still unavailable for many early videos.
