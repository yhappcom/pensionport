## Capability boundaries

- Text-source review and mapping: completed within the documented source scope.
- Independent checks: general rules and arithmetic verified or quarantined where evidence is inadequate.
- Educational principles: twelve concepts were classified for decision use.
- Quantitative scenario comparison: demonstrations are feasible for seven concepts, using **hypothetical assumptions**, not return forecasts.
- Five concepts are limited to legal, account, contract or client-information screening.
- Full investor-specific suitability, asset allocation, securities selection, tax optimization, buy/sell execution approval: **not demonstrated**.
- Full video/audio corroboration: **not performed**. Any source-specific interpretation dependent on inaccessible media remains unverified.

## Standard for subsequent research

1. Verify available source integrity and audit all relevant content regions.
2. Map each atomic statement to a principle or a documented exclusion/open reason.
3. Verify time-sensitive tax and investment rules against authoritative sources and effective dates.
4. Recalculate scenario formulas with stated assumptions; distinguish account value, contributions, taxable basis and actual withdrawals.
5. Record applicable client inputs, alternatives, costs, risks, exceptions and unsupported use.
6. Separate **study-level closure**, **individual principle verification** and **personal action readiness**.
7. For decision support, add scenario sensitivity, comparison criteria and suitability-data requirements.
8. Do not promote unsupported investment illustrations to personalized trading or retirement recommendations.

## Roadmap to improve quality
- Define the client-input schema and missing-information checklist per decision.
- Add independently reproducible cross-strategy models and downside scenarios.
- Specify rejection/stop rules, account/product conditions and valid as-of dates.
- Test decision outputs on anonymized hypothetical portfolios before real use.
