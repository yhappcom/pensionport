Educational use: year-based normalized taxation, deployment scenario. Blocked: **KRW10k IRP annuity activation and contract replacement**.

**#004:** Missing historical video source date and definition of current-account-to-GDP near 20%; specific issuer financial statements and individual position/tax basis. Educational use: loss arithmetic and hypothetical switching break-even. Blocked: **specific buy/hold/sell action or forecast based on unverified macro ratio**.

**#005:** Missing actual youth savings offer and eligibility; individual take-home pay/debt/expenses; specific TDF fees, glide path and investment plan. Educational use: budget constraints and emergency reserve illustration. Blocked: **personal account split or TDF purchase**.

**Closeout guard:** Unavailable personal/archival proof remains the source of OPEN status. Reopen only affected claims upon new primary evidence, relevant rule changes, or model failure; do not reprocess all five videos or duplicate the existing 62 claims.
