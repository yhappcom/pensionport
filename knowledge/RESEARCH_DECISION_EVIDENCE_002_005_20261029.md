## Findings that change a calculation or future interpretation

**Study 003 — investment pacing:** At 00:10:13–00:10:43 the SRT compares KRW200m with a KRW70m/year salary (2.857 years), then suggests KRW4.5m in monthly purchases. The monthly schedule instead uses 44.444 months (~3.70 years; 45 instalments with a partial final month). At 36 months it invests KRW162m and leaves KRW38m before fees and returns. These are **different illustrative schedules**, not evidence that a particular one is optimal. The independent KRW300m / KRW5m monthly example is 60 months.

**Study 004 — loss recovery:** A 40% paper loss requires a 66.67% rise from the reduced price to break even; a 50% loss requires a 100% rise. This is **new illustrative mathematics, not a source-video statement**, and does not prove a security will recover in three years. Avoid unconditional app deletion, buy, sell or hold advice.

**Study 005 — saving and TDF:** The illustrative monthly KRW500k youth savings + KRW400k ISA + KRW100k pension equals KRW1m, not an optimal allocation. A 30-year-old in 2026 retiring at 65 implies approximately 2061; the 2060 target-date label does not guarantee capital or desired allocation.

**Study 002 — account and product scope:** The current NTS reference documents eligible pension contribution ceilings of KRW6m (pension savings) and KRW9m (with eligible retirement plan), distinct from tax refund amounts. KRX gold trades differ from physical withdrawals subject to 10% VAT. BoK's 3.00% base-rate date (2026-08-27) is not a particular CMA yield.
