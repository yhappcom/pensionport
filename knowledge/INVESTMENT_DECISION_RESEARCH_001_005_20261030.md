## Reproducible quantitative highlights
- **#1:** KRW300k per month at nominal 7% compounded monthly: 84 months KRW32.40m; 120 months KRW51.93m. KRW800m account is not KRW800m tax-excluded principal. Assuming all KRW580m of source contributions/transfers were eligible as non-taxable basis, KRW4m/month nominally corresponds to 145 months, not 16 years, and real eligibility may make the basis smaller. A KRW1m pension at 65 vs KRW1.36m at 70: discounted 2% at death age 85 makes immediate payments more valuable by KRW6.34m; at age 90 delayed payments more valuable by KRW7.49m, within the *fictional* model, not personal NPS forecasts.
- **#2:** On an invented 100→110 twelve-month path with a mid-year dip, immediate investment gains 10% while equal monthly DCA with idle-cash interest gains 24.37%. On a monotonic 100→124 path, immediate gains 24% and DCA gains 13.07%. Neither strategy is universally optimal.
- **#3:** KRW200m / KRW70m annual salary=2.86 years; KRW200m / KRW4.5m monthly buying=44.44 months (3.70 years). A 60-month hypothetical constant +7% market path gives KRW280.51m for immediate investment vs KRW257.11m for KRW4.5m staged buying; at −7% the results are KRW139.14m vs KRW166.09m, assuming 2% nominal interest on unused cash and no tax/fees.
- **#4:** Losses of 40% and 50% require gains of 66.67% and 100% respectively just to recover purchase cost; this does not predict issuer recovery or justify holding.
- **#5:** Monthly 50/40/10 source illustration sums to KRW1m, but a hypothetical KRW3m take-home income less KRW2m costs and KRW400k monthly debt leaves only KRW600k. TDF saving KRW100k monthly for 30 years at a *fictional* gross 6% produces KRW94.88m at 0.3% annual fee and KRW83.23m at 1% fee; no future return or actual fund suitability guarantee.
